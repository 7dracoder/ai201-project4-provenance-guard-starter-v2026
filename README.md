# Provenance Guard

## What This Does

1. POST /submit receives JSON containing text and creator_id.
2. The route checks that both are nonempty strings and text is at most 10,000 characters.
3. detector.model_signal reads the text with a local GPT-2 language model and measures predictability.
4. stylometry.style_signal measures sentence length spread, word variety, and punctuation density.
5. scoring.combine_signals combines the two scores into a number from 0 to 1.
6. scoring.score_to_label selects a full sentence label, and audit.log_decision records the decision with a unique content_id.
7. The response returns content_id, guess, confidence, label, model_score, style_score, and status. A writer can send content_id and reasoning to POST /appeal; the service records under_review and confirms without rescoring.

A human writer can be wrongly flagged. For example, a score of 0.90 receives the AI label below, but the writer can appeal using the returned content_id. The original decision stays in the log beside the appeal.

## Signals and Scoring

Planned rule, written before implementation: scoring.py::combine_signals uses 0.7 * model_score + 0.3 * style_score. Weights live in config.py. Higher means more likely AI. A model score of 0.9 and style score of 0.1 combine to 0.66, which remains unsure.

The model signal in detector.py::model_signal measures how predictable words are in context to GPT-2. Generated text can be predictable, but ordinary human writing can be too. It cannot prove authorship and only reads the first 400 tokens.

The style signal in stylometry.py::style_signal measures shape without meaning. Regular sentence lengths, repeated vocabulary, and little punctuation increase its AI score. It cannot understand content; typos, quotations, text length, and deliberate sentence changes can fool it.

Planned style rule: average 1 - min(sentence-length coefficient of variation, 1), 1 - unique-word ratio, and 1 - min(punctuation density / 0.1, 1). Spread is sample standard deviation divided by mean sentence word count. Punctuation density is ASCII punctuation characters divided by total characters. Fewer than two sentences or ten words gives a neutral style score of 0.5.

## Label Variants

| Guess | Combined score | Exact label |
|---|---|---|
| human | 0 <= score <= 0.20 | We think this was written by a person, but this estimate is not proof of authorship. |
| unsure | 0.20 < score < 0.80 | We are unsure who wrote this text. This estimate should not be treated as an accusation. You can request a review. |
| ai | 0.80 <= score <= 1 | We think this may have been generated by AI, but we could be wrong. You can request a review if you wrote it yourself. |

The wide unsure range is intended to reduce wrong accusations. The confidence field is the combined AI score, rather than the probability that a guess is correct.
